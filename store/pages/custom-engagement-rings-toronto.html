Choose the centre stone

Begin with shape and overall look, then compare size, proportions, quality details, origin preference, and budget. Natural and lab-grown diamonds can both be considered. Read the natural vs lab-grown diamond guide before comparing individual stones. A coloured gemstone can also become the centre of the design.

Choose a setting for real life

A low or high profile, prong style, halo, side stones, and band width affect both appearance and wearability. Share the wearer’s routine so the design direction can account for comfort and practicality as well as beauty.

Choose the metal and finishing

Yellow, white, and rose gold each change how the stone and setting read. Finish, band shape, and matching-band plans should be considered together so the ring feels resolved from every angle.

Confirm budget and timing

The final price depends on the stone, metal, setting, size, and complexity. Made-to-order timing is confirmed for the specific project. If the ring is tied to a date, include that date in the first request.
