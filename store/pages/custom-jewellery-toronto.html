What can be customized?

Depending on the piece, decisions may include natural or lab-grown diamonds, coloured gemstones, 14K or 18K gold, metal colour, centre-stone shape, setting style, halo or side stones, band profile, engraving, length, clasp, and overall proportions.

What should I prepare?

A few reference images and a realistic budget range are useful, but you do not need a complete design. Explain what you like about each reference and how the piece will be worn. That gives the consultation a clearer starting point.

How are price and timing confirmed?

Custom jewellery depends on the final stone, metal, size, complexity, and sourcing. Price and timing are therefore confirmed for the individual project before you commit. Use the request form to share the details needed for an informed reply.
