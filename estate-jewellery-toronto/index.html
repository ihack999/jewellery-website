What information should I request?

Ask for the measurements, materials, gemstone details, condition, service history where relevant, and any available documentation. For watches and branded pieces, request the exact reference and authentication information available for that item.

Are all estate pieces shown online?

The online edit represents selected current pieces. If you are searching for a particular style, category, or budget, describe it in your inquiry so the team can confirm whether an adjacent piece is available.

Why are some prices available only by request?

Some one-of-a-kind pieces require a direct conversation to confirm current availability and the details associated with that specific item. Publicly priced pieces display their price on the collection and product pages.
