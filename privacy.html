Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Toronto Jewels Curation handles information submitted through this website.

Information you choose to provide

When you submit a contact, appointment, private inquiry, custom jewellery, design request, or checkout, you may provide your name, email address, telephone number, shipping or billing address, budget, timing, jewellery preferences, measurements, notes, and reference files. Please do not upload sensitive identity, financial, or medical documents.

How information is used

Submitted information is used to process an order or payment, review and respond to your request, discuss design or sourcing options, prepare next steps, provide requested service, prevent misuse, and maintain necessary business records.

Website storage and saved preferences

The site may store functional data in your browser, such as items in your shopping bag, a form draft, saved pieces, comparison choices, recently viewed products, or a custom design preview state. This local storage stays on your device unless you submit the information. You can clear it through your browser settings.

Service providers

The website is hosted through Netlify. Checkout and payment information is processed securely by Stripe; Toronto Jewels Curation does not receive your full card number. Form submissions may be processed by Netlify, and an inquiry notification may be sent through Twilio when that service is configured. The custom design preview’s optional image-generation feature may send the design prompt to OpenAI when you request a generated image. Google Fonts may receive basic connection information when fonts load. These providers process data under their own terms and privacy practices.

Sharing, retention, and security

Information is shared only with service providers or collaborators needed to operate the website, process your order, and respond to the request, or when required by law. Information is retained only as long as reasonably needed for the inquiry, transaction, project, recordkeeping, security, or legal obligations. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices

You may ask about personal information associated with your inquiry or order, request a correction, or request deletion where applicable. Some transaction records may need to be retained for legal, security, or legitimate business reasons.

Contact

For a privacy question or request, email info@torontojewelscuration.com. If this policy changes materially, the effective date at the top of this page will be updated.
